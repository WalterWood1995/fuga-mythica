隐私说明

最后更新:2026年8月29日

我们不收集任何个人数据

Fuga Mythica 没有服务器,也没有账号系统。游戏不会把任何信息发送给我们 —— 因为没有可以接收信息的地方。我们看不到你是谁、玩了什么、玩得怎么样。

存在你设备上的东西

你在游戏里创建的冒险者档案(昵称、密码的哈希值、年龄段、性别选项、闯关进度、错词记录、成绩)只写在这台设备的浏览器本地存储(localStorage)里。密码以 SHA-256 哈希保存,原文不会被存下。这些数据不会离开你的设备。

清除浏览器网站数据、或在游戏里点「清除进度重新开始」,这些内容会被永久删除,我们无法恢复。想留底请到「设置 → 存档备份」导出文件自行保管。

会向外部发出的请求

  • Wikimedia Commons(commons.wikimedia.org)—— 游戏里的古典艺术插图从这里读取。你的设备会因此向 Wikimedia 发出图片请求。
  • Google Fonts(fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com)—— 载入两款标题字体。

这两项由各自的服务方按其隐私政策处理请求日志。我们不向它们发送任何关于你的信息,也无法读取这些日志。若在「设置」里下载了离线包,图片会缓存在本机,之后不再重复请求。

没有广告、没有统计、没有第三方追踪

游戏不含广告 SDK、分析工具、追踪像素或社交插件,也不使用 Cookie 做识别。

关于儿童

本游戏为儿童学习拉丁语而做。注册时可填的年龄与性别为可选项,仅用于在本机挑选题目难度和称呼方式,不会被上传。由于不存在数据回传,我们不掌握任何儿童个人信息。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

We collect nothing

Fuga Mythica has no server and no online accounts. The game sends us no information, because there is nowhere for it to send information to. We cannot see who you are, what you played, or how you did.

What stays on your device

The adventurer profiles you create — nickname, a hash of the password, optional age and gender, level progress, missed words and scores — are written only to this device's browser storage (localStorage). Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes; the password itself is never kept. None of this leaves your device.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using "Start over" in the game, deletes it permanently; we cannot restore it. Use Settings → Save backup to export a copy you keep yourself.

Outside requests the game makes

  • Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) — the classical artwork is loaded from there, so your device requests images from Wikimedia.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — two display typefaces.

Those services handle their own request logs under their own privacy policies. We send them nothing about you and cannot read those logs. If you download the offline pack in Settings, the images are cached locally and are not requested again.

No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking

The game contains no advertising SDK, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no social plugins, and uses no identifying cookies.

Children

This game is made for children learning Latin. The optional age and gender fields at sign-up are used only on the device, to pick question difficulty and forms of address; they are never uploaded. Because nothing is transmitted, we hold no personal information about any child.
